The struggle behind the numbers

01

Struggle

Cost per lead sat near ₹1,000 on competitive head terms.

→

What I changed

Moved Ads to low-competition, high-demand secondary keywords; kept main business terms for SEO and AEO.

Evidence

Avg. CPC ₹6.15 and cost per conversion ₹589.

02

Struggle

Leads arrived but sales said they weren’t budgeted.

→

What I changed

Reviewed sales calls and Clarity sessions: buyers only knew per-sq-ft pricing. Added BOQ education, sample BOQs and a dedicated landing page.

Evidence

CTR 4.73%, with more direct calls and lead forms.

03

Struggle

One message for every part of Chennai.

→

What I changed

Grouped pincodes into premium, salaried, business and IT areas, with ad copy written for each.

Evidence

Conversions 440.3 from 42,108 clicks.

04

Struggle

Search alone limited reach and bids kept climbing.

→

What I changed

Added Performance Max with video and custom segments built from competitor terms and keywords.

Evidence

186 calls from ads recorded in the account.

The struggle behind the numbers

01

Struggle

New brand, WhatsApp-lead campaign: leads were mostly Hindi-speaking and irrelevant.

→

What I changed

Studied competitors in the Meta Ad Library (they used WhatsApp and lead forms), then their copy, landing pages and creative formats. Switched to lead forms.

Evidence

2,582 form leads at ₹187.03 each.

02

Struggle

Hindi calls and leads from other cities.

→

What I changed

Targeted by pincode and area: premium buyers, business owners and IT professionals, with one ad set per area.

Evidence

Eight area-based ad sets delivered 178 to 249 leads each.

03

Struggle

Guessing which message would land.

→

What I changed

Built five video angles (BOQ vs per-sq-ft education, informational, benefit-led). Every video: hook, urgency, then contact CTA.

Evidence

Best ad set, a video: ₹117.08 per lead, 2.29% CTR.

04

Struggle

Costs differed widely between audiences.

→

What I changed

Compared ad sets side by side. Most ran at the same ₹200 daily budget, so cost per lead was directly comparable. Added a set from sales-team location data.

Evidence

₹117 to ₹494 per lead across the 13 ad sets shown.

Jargon, decoded

CPL · cost per leadWhat one enquiry costs: spend ÷ leads.
Cost per conversionWhat one tracked action costs, such as a call or form.
CTR · click-through rateHow many people who see an ad click it.
CPC · cost per clickWhat one visit from an ad costs.
BOQ · bill of quantitiesAn itemised construction quote. Buyers expected a simple per-sq-ft price.
PMax · Performance MaxGoogle’s campaign type that runs across Search, YouTube, Display and Maps.
SERP · results pageThe Google page shown for a search. Page one is what matters.
AEO · answer engine optimisationWriting pages so AI assistants quote them in their answers.

Project 1
Paid searchPerformance Max

Construction services, Chennai

Leads came in. Sales kept saying “not budgeted.”

01

Struggle

Cost per lead stayed high and the leads that arrived lacked budget. Buyers compared per-sq-ft rates only, so a BOQ-based quote felt expensive and confusing.

02

Discussion

I sat with sales, listened to call language and watched session recordings. The gap wasn’t traffic: buyers didn’t understand a BOQ, and competitor keywords were crowding the bids.

03

Testing

Low-competition secondary keywords in Ads, core terms kept for SEO. Pincode clusters (premium, salaried, business, IT) with tailored copy. Performance Max with video and competitor-term segments. BOQ explainer videos and sample BOQs.

04

Result

−41%cost per conversion
4.7%CTR, Search + PMax
↑direct calls and lead forms

Project 3
Meta AdsLocal SEOAI search

Construction services, Madurai

Every project came from referrals. Online, the brand didn’t exist.

01

Struggle

The builder ran only on referrals in Madurai. No search visibility and no lead flow beyond word of mouth, so they needed an online presence and a steady source of leads.

02

Discussion

Before launch I studied the audience and local competitors, then mapped Madurai by pincode. Campaigns started on a low test budget. Early leads were mostly people wanting a ready-built home, or irrelevant enquiries.

03

Testing

Tested different pincodes, multiple creatives and several primary texts, and local offers for a secondary premium audience. Built SEO pages for the cost and “best builder” searches Madurai buyers make.

04

Result

143leads at ₹177 each
9page-one Google queries in 3 months
#1“best construction companies in madurai”

Video brought the best-quality leads from in and around Madurai. The top video ad reached a 1.78% CTR at ₹147 per lead.

Project 6
B2B eventsLinkedIn · Google · Meta

Fintech and blockchain summits

Leads arrived. Registrations didn’t.

01

Struggle

Early conversion-stage campaigns produced leads but few registrations and sponsor or delegate passes.

02

Discussion

Competitor analysis: channel mix, geography, demographics and audience overlap, paid keywords and CPCs, creatives, ad-library checks. Their paid footprint was small and no LinkedIn or Meta ads showed. I also mined sales calls for the words buyers used.

03

Testing

A 60/20/20 split across LinkedIn, Google and Meta, launched months ahead: awareness, then traffic, then conversion. Image and video rotation, with copy rewritten around urgency, networking and business opportunity.

04

Result

Project 7
Paid + organicUtility-led campaign

Non-profit tax-saving tools

A tax calculator that taught a charity how to be found.

01

Struggle

Low brand awareness, a bounce rate near 76% and paid traffic that didn’t act.

02

Discussion

Search terms about saving tax by donating showed real demand, and “income tax calculator” drew about 301K searches a month.

03

Testing

A slider-based calculator, a tax-saving game and three blog pages. GTM event tracking on every button, exact and phrase match, negatives, placement exclusions and retargeting.

04

Result

+102%monthly visitors, same budget
+616%site interactions
−69%CPC
2.4→28%search CTR

What didn’t work: blog pages on niche donation terms. Only the general tax-saving topic performed, and donor conversions needed longer than the campaign window.
